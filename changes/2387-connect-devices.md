## en

- Settings → Devices has a "Connect without Tailscale" section: sign in to a DeepSeekBot account, confirm the code in the browser, then see the Connect address and its state, or sign out ([#2387](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2387)).
