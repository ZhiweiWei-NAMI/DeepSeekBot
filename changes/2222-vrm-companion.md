## en

- The VRM character sits higher relative to the desk with elbows on the armrests and forearms level, turns its wrist to rest its chin on its knuckles, and now and then picks up its phone to look at it. Speech bubbles show shorter pages and no longer show Markdown symbols or table rules ([#2222](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2222)).
- A VRM reply that follows tool calls is no longer cut off after its first character when the status connection blips; speech pauses and resumes instead. Long replies stay inside the panel ([#2222](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2222)).
- The Bot status above the message box, at the end of the message list (on the web and in the mobile app), the sidebar and the VRM character no longer flicker between thinking and a tool call: a short tool call stays on thinking, a new state must hold for a moment before it shows, and tool details change at most every 1.5 seconds, while waiting, blocked and waking from idle still show at once. When the Bot picks up a message, the VRM character notices it: the phone on the desk lights up and buzzes, an envelope pops out of its screen and floats there, an exclamation mark pops above the character's head and it glances at the phone before getting to work. Typing is now fast and steady while working, slow and intermittent while thinking and stops at idle, easing in and out instead of switching on and off. Resting a chin now turns the character toward you with a loose fist under the chin, stretching opens and spreads the fingers with the wrists bent back, and resting fingers curl progressively from index to little finger instead of keeping the typing shape. Day and night follow your system clock with sunrise and sunset worked out for today's date and your time zone, shown under **Scene → Time** ([#2222](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2222)).
- Long replies in the VRM panel now appear one sentence at a time with a pause after each. While the Bot talks, the character puts its cup down, turns toward you and keeps facing you through the pauses, nodding slightly, and blinks at natural intervals. Dragging the panel width no longer flashes white, the scene runs at up to 60 fps (it was stuck near 26 fps on high-refresh screens) and lowers its resolution on slow machines ([#2222](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2222)).
- All VRM panel controls now live in the panel header as icon buttons, like the other panels, instead of over the 3D scene: a camera-tour toggle, a **Character** menu, a **Scene** menu for view, time, weather, look and movement, and a pin. The new **Auto camera tour** slowly cycles the camera through desk, portrait, wide, over-the-shoulder and room shots, pausing while you drag ([#2222](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2222)).
- **Change character…** in the VRM panel replaces the character with a `.vrm` file from your computer (VRoid/VRM 0.x or VRM 1.0). The file is remembered in this browser for that Bot and never uploaded, the same typing, gestures, expressions and speech bubble apply to it, and **Use default** switches back ([#2222](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2222)).
- When the Bot speaks, its words now appear in a speech bubble above the character's head, styled like the Bot avatar's bubble, with the tail following the head as the camera moves. Typing hands now rest on the keys instead of sinking into the keyboard, typing comes in natural bursts, head turns and idle gestures ease in and out, and the clouds are soft and misty instead of blocky ([#2305](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2305)).
- While typing, the character now strikes keys with individual fingers instead of bobbing its palms. The room surfaces have a soft painted brush texture with smoother toon shading, and the room is lit by several zoned lights: a wall sconce, a glow inside the book hutch, an LED strip under the wall shelf, warm bounce light and sunbeams from the window at sunset. New details include cables to the wall outlet, an air conditioner with a swinging louver, a basket of blankets, slippers, a floor cushion and a printed mug ([#2305](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2305)).
- The default character is now VRoid's AvatarSample_A, and the room is rebuilt around an L-shaped desk with a turntable, laptop, wall shelf and corkboard on the side wing. A hanging plant, a firefly jar, wall prints, a desk calendar showing today's date and labelled book spines make the room denser, the character gets a warm or cool rim light from the time of day, and the desk lamp no longer washes out the hands ([#2305](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2305)).
- A new **Lo-fi look**, on by default, gives the room the hand-drawn feel of Chill with You: outlines, a soft glow around lights, halftone shadows, a warm sunset and cool night grade, and a vignette; **Plain** turns it off. The sun now has a ringed halo and birds occasionally fly past the window ([#2305](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2305)).
- Scene controls can switch the weather to rain, with streaks outside, an overcast sky and droplets running down the window; clouds are drawn with toon shading lit from the sun or moon. The monitor shows what the Bot is doing (typing code, a scrolling log, a dialog, or a music player at idle) and its glow lights the desk at night, and an open book turns its pages while loose papers and dust move in the breeze ([#2305](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2305)).
- The desk view is recomposed after Chill with You: the monitor stands in front of the character, the camera sits beside the desk for a three-quarter view, the sunset is warmer and redder, and VRoid models load with the same seated choreography ([#2305](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2305)).
- The room is now furnished like a lived-in study corner (a book hutch, desk clutter, a drawer cabinet, a rug, a poster, a clock showing local time, a floor lamp and string lights that brighten at night), and Scene controls can load your own `.vrm` file, including VRoid models, for the session without uploading it ([#2305](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2305)).
- Room objects now follow real-world proportions (a smaller cup, keyboard, monitor and lamp) in a low-poly, flat toon-shaded style with a blue night and a warm desk-lamp pool ([#2305](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2305)).
- While the Bot is idle, the seated character now occasionally looks toward the window, stretches, rests its chin on a hand, sips from its cup or glances at you, and returns to the keyboard as soon as the Bot gets to work ([#2305](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2305)).
- The character's face now follows what the Bot is doing: relaxed at idle, a thoughtful upward look while thinking, focused while working, an expectant smile while waiting and a troubled look when blocked, easing between them and warming up while speaking ([#2304](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2304)).
- The room now has a framed window in a plaster wall with linen curtains, soft drawn clouds and a glowing sun, a darker night lit by the desk lamp, and a desk view that keeps the lamp, cup and character in frame. MToon/lilToon remains a reversible comparison, scene choices survive the view reloading, and loading no longer looks like an error; final visual acceptance remains pending ([#2222](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2222)).

- Added an independently installable VRM character Panel with live Bot Activity, text-paced speaking, and persistent fixed-Bot instances; the first slice uses a licensed sample model and produces no audio ([#2222](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2222), [developer guide](../docs/panel-plugin.md)).
- The character now shares a full-panel 3D room: seated keyboard contact, stand/walk/return controls, camera views, and a spatial sky with drifting clouds and local-time lighting. Scene controls are overlays; movement and room time remain local presentation, independent of Bot Activity ([#2222](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2222)).

## zh

- VRM 角色相对桌面坐得更高：手肘搭在扶手上，前臂放平；托下巴时手腕会转过来，用指节托住下巴；闲着时偶尔会拿起手机看一会儿。说话气泡每页更短，也不再显示 Markdown 符号和表格分隔线（[#2222](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2222)）。
- 调用过工具的回复不会再在状态连接短暂中断时只显示第一个字就被清掉，改为暂停后接着说；长回复的气泡不再超出面板顶部（[#2222](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2222)）。
- 消息框上方和消息列表末尾的 Bot 状态（网页和手机 App）、侧边栏和 VRM 角色不再在“思考”和工具调用之间来回闪：很短的工具调用会继续显示为思考，新状态要稳定一小会儿才切换，工具详情最快每 1.5 秒更新一次；等待、受阻和从空闲醒来仍会立即显示。Bot 接到消息时，VRM 角色会注意到：桌上的手机亮屏并震动，一封信从手机屏幕里弹出来悬浮一会儿，角色头顶弹出感叹号，角色先看一眼手机再开始干活。打字节奏随状态变化：工作时快而连贯，思考时慢而断续，空闲时停下，并且平滑地开始和停止，不再一下开一下关。托腮时转向你，手松松握拳托住下巴，伸懒腰时手指张开、手腕后翻，平时手指从食指到小指自然弯曲，不再一直是打字手型。昼夜跟随系统时间，日出日落按当天日期和所在时区计算，可在 **Scene → Time** 下看到（[#2222](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2222)）。
- VRM 面板里的长回复改为一句一句显示，每句说完停顿一下。Bot 说话时，角色会先放下杯子，再转身看向你，停顿时也保持面向你并轻轻点头；眨眼间隔更自然。拖动 panel 宽度时不再闪白；画面最高 60 fps（之前在高刷新率屏幕上只有约 26 fps），在较慢的机器上会自动降低分辨率（[#2222](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2222)）。
- VRM 面板的控件全部移到面板标题栏，和其他面板一样使用图标按钮，不再覆盖在 3D 画面上：轮换机位开关、**Character** 人物菜单、包含视角／时间／天气／画面风格／动作的 **Scene** 菜单，以及固定按钮。新增的 **Auto camera tour**，镜头会在桌边、近景、远景、肩后和房间全景之间缓慢轮换，拖动画面时暂停（[#2222](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2222)）。
- VRM 面板新增 **Change character…**，可以用电脑上的 `.vrm` 文件（VRoid/VRM 0.x 或 VRM 1.0）替换人物。文件按 Bot 保存在当前浏览器里，不会上传；打字、小动作、表情和说话气泡同样适用于新人物；**Use default** 恢复默认人物（[#2222](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2222)）。
- Bot 说话时，文字会以对话气泡显示在角色头顶，样式与 Bot 头像的气泡一致，气泡尾巴随镜头跟住头部。打字时手放在按键上，不再陷进键盘，敲击带有自然的快慢节奏；转头和闲置动作的起止更平滑；云改成柔和的云雾，不再是明显的块状（[#2305](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2305)）。
- 打字时角色改为逐根手指敲击按键，不再是手掌上下起伏。房间表面加入柔和的手绘笔触质感，toon 明暗更平滑；房间改由多处分区灯光照亮：墙上壁灯、书架内的暖光、置物架下的 LED 灯带，日落时还有窗边的暖色反光和光束。新增细节包括连到墙上插座的线缆、扫风叶片会摆动的空调、装着毯子的编织篮、拖鞋、地上坐垫和印花马克杯（[#2305](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2305)）。
- 默认角色换成 VRoid 的 AvatarSample_A；房间改为 L 型书桌，侧桌上有唱片机、笔记本电脑、墙上置物架和软木留言板。吊篮绿植、萤火虫玻璃罐、墙上小画、显示当天日期的台历和带书标的书脊让画面更丰富；角色会随时间带上暖色或冷色的轮廓光，台灯也不再把手照得过曝（[#2305](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2305)）。
- 新增默认开启的 **Lo-fi look**，让房间更接近 Chill with You 的手绘感：物体描边、灯光柔光、暗部网点、日落暖红与夜晚冷蓝的调色以及暗角；选择 **Plain** 可以关闭。太阳带上了光环，窗外偶尔有鸟飞过（[#2305](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2305)）。
- Scene controls 可以切换为雨天：窗外下雨、天空转阴、雨滴沿玻璃滑落；云层改为随太阳或月亮方向受光的 toon 着色。显示器内容跟随 Bot 的状态（敲代码、滚动日志、对话框，空闲时是音乐播放器），入夜后屏幕光会照亮桌面；桌上翻开的书会自己翻页，纸张和灰尘随窗外的风轻轻飘动（[#2305](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2305)）。
- 桌边视角参照 Chill with You 重新构图：显示器放到角色正前方，镜头移到桌边侧前方呈 3/4 视角，日落更暖更红；VRoid 模型也能以同样的坐姿和动作载入（[#2305](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2305)）。
- 房间布置得更有生活感：书架、桌面小物、抽屉柜、地毯、海报、显示本地时间的挂钟，以及入夜会亮起的落地灯和窗边彩灯；Scene controls 还可以打开本机的 `.vrm` 文件（包括 VRoid 模型），仅在本次会话中使用，不会上传（[#2305](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2305)）。
- 房间物件改为真实世界比例（更小的杯子、键盘、显示器和台灯），采用 low poly 平涂 toon 着色；夜晚为蓝调，由台灯在桌面投下暖光（[#2305](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2305)）。
- Bot 空闲时，坐在桌前的角色会不时看向窗外、伸懒腰、托腮、端起杯子喝一口或看你一眼；Bot 一开始工作就平滑回到键盘（[#2305](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2305)）。
- 角色的表情现在跟随 Bot 的状态：空闲时放松，思考时视线上移、若有所思，工作时专注，等待时带着期待的微笑，受阻时显得困扰；表情之间平滑过渡，说话时更柔和明亮（[#2304](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2304)）。
- 房间改为灰泥墙上的窗框与亚麻窗帘、柔和的绘制云层和发光太阳；夜晚整体变暗，由台灯照亮桌面；桌边视角同时保留台灯、杯子和角色。MToon／lilToon 仍可切换对比，视图重新加载后保留场景选择，加载状态不再显示为错误；最终视觉验收尚未完成（[#2222](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2222)）。

- 新增可独立安装的 VRM 角色面板，呈现 Bot 实时 Activity、文字节奏说话和可保存的固定 Bot 实例；首片采用已授权示例模型，不产生音频（[#2222](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2222)，[开发指南](../docs/panel-plugin.md)）。
- 角色现在置于铺满面板的三维房间：支持坐姿键盘接触、起身／走动／回座、镜头切换，以及有纵深的动态云层和本机时间光照。控件覆盖在画面上，角色移动和房间时间仅属于本地呈现，不改变 Bot Activity（[#2222](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2222)）。
